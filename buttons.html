Add Tulpar to your website

Put a Tulpar button on your site and visitors can subscribe to your channel in one tap. It takes two minutes and works on any website.

1. Find your handle

Every channel on Tulpar has a handle, like @talasfilms or @bereke. Open Tulpar, search for your business and copy the handle from its channel page. Channels are for businesses, organisations, media and sports clubs, not personal accounts.

No channel yet? Write to channels@tulpar.app with your business name, address and website, and we will set one up, usually within a week.

2. Your link

Your subscribe link is your handle added to this address:

https://tulparapp.com/app/#sub=yourhandle

When someone opens it, Tulpar shows your channel with a Subscribe button. On an Android phone with the Tulpar app installed it opens in the app; everywhere else it opens in the browser, and nobody needs to sign in first.

3. Choose a button

Type your handle, pick a style and copy the code into your page, where you keep your other social links (usually the footer or the contact page).

Using the Tulpar icon

        
  • Use the icon as it is: the white tulpar on the turquoise tile. Don't change its colours, rotate it or put text inside the tile.
  • Keep a little space around it, at least a quarter of its width.
  • Don't make it smaller than 20 pixels.
  • Say "Subscribe on Tulpar" or "@yourhandle on Tulpar"; please don't suggest that Tulpar endorses your business.
